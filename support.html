Restore a purchase

On the paywall, tap “Already bought it? Restore your purchase.” After you have access, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.

What the app does not do

No account, no cloud backup of your phrases, no ads. Microphone is used only when you choose to record a speaking practice.
