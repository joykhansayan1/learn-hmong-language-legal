Privacy Policy

Learn Hmong Language by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy. Effective 26 August 2026. Contact: dmjks120@gmail.com.

What this app is

Learn Hmong Language is a dual-track language app for White Hmong (Hmoob Dawb) and Green Hmong / Mong Leng (Moob Leeg). The two varieties are never merged. Free sample lessons are available; the full course is unlocked through Apple In-App Purchase. There is no account.

Data we do not collect

We do not create an account. We do not upload your phrases, recordings, family notes, or progress. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Contacts, photos, and location are not requested.

What stays on your device

Your variety track, session choices, saved phrases, lesson progress, and any practice recordings you make stay in on-device storage. Practice recordings are made only when you tap Record in a speaking exercise so you can play them back next to the lesson audio. You can delete all local data in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may associate the purchase with your Apple Account under Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional usage counts

Anonymous usage counts are off by default. If you turn them on, they never include a phrase, a recording, or anything that identifies you. This version of the app does not transmit those counts to a server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
